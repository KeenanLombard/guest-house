# Asset notes

Images were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. These show a fictional guest house and are not documentary photographs of a real property. Originals remain in the image generation output directory; the site uses optimized, locally stored JPEGs.

All prompts use the `photorealistic-natural` category and request editorial architectural travel photography, natural light, credible materials, muted coastal colours, no text, no collage and no people.

## `assets/coast.jpg`

Prompt: An ultra-wide premium architectural travel photograph for the full-bleed hero of a luxury guest house website. A fictional intimate boutique guest house on the coastline at Wilderness, Western Cape, South Africa. View from a sheltered pale limestone terrace towards a vast empty golden beach and the blue-green Indian Ocean with natural lines of white surf. Right third shows part of the white plaster guest house, warm timber sliding doors, trailing dune grasses and a single linen sun lounger. Left two thirds are open ocean and sky with space for white typography. Coastal hills disappear towards the left horizon. South African Garden Route terrain, no tropical palms. Warm late-afternoon sunlight, faded sea blue, muted sage, honey sand, tactile realistic surfaces, tranquil luxury, believable architectural photography rather than CGI. Panoramic landscape 2.4:1 composition. No text, people, watermark or collage.
